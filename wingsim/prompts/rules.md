RULES SUMMARY (Wingspan, core set)

Goal: score points over 4 rounds. Points come from the printed points on birds you have played, 1 per egg on your birds, 1 per food cached on your birds, 1 per card tucked behind your birds, your bonus cards, and the round goals.

Your mat has 3 habitats (forest, grassland, wetland), each a row of up to 5 birds filled from the left.

Rounds and turns: players take turns clockwise. Each player gets 8 turns in round 1, then 7, 6 and 5. On your turn you take exactly one of four actions:

1. Play a bird from your hand into a habitat it can live in. Pay its food cost, plus an egg cost for the column: 0 eggs for the 1st bird in a row, 1 egg for the 2nd or 3rd, 2 eggs for the 4th or 5th (eggs are removed from your own birds). Any 2 food can stand in for 1 food you lack. A "when played" (white) power may be used once, now.
2. Gain food (forest): take food dice from the birdfeeder. The number depends on how many birds are in your forest: 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3 for 0 to 5 birds. Then you may use the brown powers of your forest birds, from right to left.
3. Lay eggs (grassland): lay 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4 eggs for 0 to 5 grassland birds, on any of your birds that have room (each bird has an egg limit). Then you may use your grassland birds' brown powers, right to left.
4. Draw cards (wetland): draw 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3 cards for 0 to 5 wetland birds, each from the face-up tray or the top of the deck. Then you may use your wetland birds' brown powers, right to left.

With 1, 3 or 5 birds in a row, its action also offers a trade for one extra: discard a card for 1 more food (forest), spend a food for 1 more egg (grassland), or remove an egg for 1 more card (wetland).

Birdfeeder: 5 dice. Taking a die gives that food; the invertebrate/seed face gives either. When the feeder is empty all 5 dice are rerolled. If all dice left show the same face, you may reroll all 5 before taking food.

Powers: brown powers may be used every time you take that row's action. White powers happen once, when the bird is played. Pink powers may be used once between your own turns, when another player does what the power names. All powers are optional. Cached food and tucked cards stay on the bird and cannot be spent.

Round goals: each round has a goal that counts something on your mat. Normally a player scores 1 point per item counted, up to 5, at the end of that round. Eggs, birds, food and cards all carry over between rounds.

Setup: you are dealt 5 birds, 5 food (one of each type) and 2 bonus cards. You keep any of the birds, discarding 1 food for each bird kept, and keep 1 bonus card.

The game ends after round 4. Leftover food and cards in hand score nothing (unless a bonus card says so).
