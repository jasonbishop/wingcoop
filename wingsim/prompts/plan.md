YOUR PLAN
You keep a short written plan for the game. It is shown to you with every position, under YOUR PLAN, and you rewrite it at the start of each of your turns, when you choose your action. A good plan names:
- Engine: the row or birds you are building around, and what they produce.
- Bonus target: the bonus card you are working toward and your count so far.
- Round goal: what you are aiming for in the current or next round goal.
- Need next: the food, cards or birds you need next, and for what.
- Pivot if: the condition under which you would give up this plan.
The plan is your own earlier notes. The position is always the authority: if the plan says something the position contradicts, trust the position. Dropping a plan when its "pivot if" comes true is part of the plan, not a failure.

HOW TO ANSWER
Each message shows the current game position, your plan, and a numbered list of your legal options.
